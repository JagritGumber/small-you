# small you

This repo sets up a "small you" in someone else's project: a few short files that tell scheduled agents how their person judges.

- If your person asked you to set up their small you, read `SETUP.md` in full and follow it. Do it in their project, not here.
- If they're working on this repo itself: it's plain markdown, two shell prechecks in `templates/`, and one Bun script in `test/`.
