# Morning brief

Read `.small-you/RULES.md` and `.small-you/scope.md` first. Then read <where the tickets live>, open pull requests on <repo> and <chat channels, or delete this>.

Write like a sharp colleague giving me a quick morning brief, not a report.

- Start with two to five plain sentences: what I should do today, in order, and why.
- Then short sections only where there's something to say: Waiting on others, Watching, PRs, Left out on purpose, Not sure. Skip empty sections.
- Give each item one or two plain sentences, ending with its source: a link, or the file and ticket ID. No field labels, no tables, no walls of bold.
- Never tell me to do something I've already done; check for proof first. Under "Left out on purpose", say what you skipped and why.
- Don't hand me work that `.small-you/scope.md` marks as cut or waiting.
- If the next step is waiting, say "wait". Never write <words that annoy me, e.g. "lock", "drive", "align" or "ensure">.
- Stay under about 25 lines unless something is on fire.
- List the questions in `.small-you/questions.md` that I haven't answered yet, one line each. Put your own open questions under "Not sure" instead of adding them to that file.
