# Interview

Ask these after reading what you can (step 2 of `SETUP.md`). Show your draft answer where you have one and ask your person to correct it. Each answer goes into the file in brackets.

1. **Project.** Which repo will the agents work in? Where do your tickets live (Linear, GitHub Issues, Jira, a file, or nowhere), and which chat channels, if any, should the brief read? *(RULES.md, brief.md)*
2. **Hands off.** What may agents do on their own, and what must go through you? The default is read-only towards people: they draft comments, reviews and messages, and you send them. *(RULES.md)*
3. **Now.** What release or milestone are you building, what are its dates, and which tickets or areas are clear to build? *(scope.md)*
4. **Cut.** What have you decided not to build, or to stop building, and when did you decide? Name the features and their tickets. This is the question that matters most: nothing in your repo or tracker says it. *(scope.md)*
5. **Waiting.** What is waiting on a spec, a decision or another person, and who? *(scope.md)*
6. **Agent work.** Which kinds of tickets are code an agent can take on alone, and which need people (decisions, specs, docs you approve, anything in review)? *(RULES.md now, playbook.md later)*
7. **Morning.** When does your day start, in which timezone, and where should the brief land: a log file, the agent's own output, or a draft you send yourself? *(steps 6 and 7)*
8. **Annoyances.** What in agent output wastes your time? For example: long reports, telling you to do what you've already done, or words like "align" and "ensure". *(brief.md, RULES.md)*

Finish with: "What's one call you made this month that an agent couldn't see from the code?" Put the answer under "Decided" in `scope.md`.
